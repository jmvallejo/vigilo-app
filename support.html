Quick Start Guide

Vigilo is designed to be ready in seconds. When you open Vigilo for the first time on a new Mac:

  1. 1-Click Starter Pack: Click "Load Starter Feeds & Radar" on the home screen. Vigilo instantly populates 4 curated technology feeds (Hacker News, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, The Verge) and configures a default AI criteria prompt.
  2. Observe On-Device Evaluation: Within moments, the local ONNX embedding engine evaluates incoming headlines against the criteria prompt and surfaces candidate cards with similarity scores.
  3. Triage with the Keyboard: Press j and k to move through cards, Enter to read in your browser, or e to dismiss.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the dual-stage evaluation engine work?

Vigilo uses a two-stage filter to keep things fast, cheap, and private:

Stage 1 (Vector Similarity): Every incoming article is converted into a 384-dimensional vector embedding on your Mac using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. It computes the cosine similarity against your radar's criteria. Unrelated articles are rejected in milliseconds with zero cloud cost.

Stage 2 (Executive Briefing): Only candidate articles that meet or exceed your similarity threshold are sent to your chosen LLM (Built-in Candle, Ollama, Gemini, or OpenAI) to generate a concise 2-sentence executive summary and urgency priority score (HIGH, MED, LOW).

Do I need an OpenAI or Google Gemini API key to use Vigilo?

No! Vigilo works out of the box with 100% on-device vector embeddings without requiring any external account or API key. For Stage 2 AI summaries, you can either:
• Use the Built-In Local AI (Candle) with quantized Gemma 3 models (downloaded directly in Settings).
• Connect to your existing local Ollama instance.
• Or optionally provide your own Google Gemini or OpenAI API key if you prefer cloud models.

What source types are supported?

Vigilo supports:
• RSS & Atom XML Feeds: Standard web feeds from any blog, publication, or podcast.
• Reddit: Any subreddit (e.g., r/technology or r/rust) or Reddit URL. Vigilo automatically connects to Reddit's Atom feed with appropriate user-agent headers and request pacing (~9 requests/min) to avoid rate limits.
• X / Twitter: User timelines (@username) or topic search queries using an optional X Developer Bearer token.

How do Radar criteria and exclusions work?

Each radar is an independent intelligence scope. You can specify:
• Criteria Prompt: Natural language description of what you are looking for (e.g., "Cybersecurity zero-day exploits, Rust compiler performance breakthroughs, or major AI research papers").
• Similarity Threshold: A slider from 0.0 to 1.0 (default 0.70 to 0.85). Higher values only surface strict matches; lower values cast a wider net.
• Exclusions: You can exclude specific feeds or entire folders from a radar if they are not relevant to that topic.

How do notifications and the system tray companion work?

When you close Vigilo's window, it continues running silently in the macOS menu bar (the monochrome radar beacon icon). When a high-priority match arrives, Vigilo fires a native macOS notification. Clicking the notification opens the article directly in your default browser. You can click the menu bar icon or press its hotkey to bring Vigilo back to the foreground anytime.

How do I backup or transfer my configuration?

Open Settings (⚙) → General. Click "Export Backup" to generate a portable JSON file containing all your sources, folders, radars, alert preferences, and exclusions. You can restore this file on any Mac using "Restore Backup" with non-destructive merge protection.

Troubleshooting & Permissions

1. Desktop Notifications are not appearing

Make sure notifications are enabled for Vigilo in macOS:

  • Open System Settings → Notifications.
  • Scroll down to Vigilo and ensure "Allow Notifications" is enabled.
  • In Vigilo's Settings → Notifications, verify whether your preference is set to "All Matches" or "High Priority Only".

2. "Initializing embedding model" banner on first launch

On first launch, Vigilo downloads the lightweight all-MiniLM-L6-v2 ONNX embedding weights (~80 MB) directly from Hugging Face into your sandbox cache. Depending on your internet speed, this takes 5–15 seconds. Once cached, the model initializes instantly on subsequent launches without network access.

3. A feed displays an amber or red warning icon

Vigilo tracks per-source health status:

  • Amber clock: The host (e.g. Reddit or Twitter) requested rate-limiting pacing. Vigilo automatically pauses and counts down before the next poll.
  • Red warning triangle: The feed URL returned a 404, 403, or invalid XML. Hover over the warning in the sidebar or Settings to view the exact error code. You can click the three dots next to the feed to edit the URL or pause the feed.

4. Launch at Login

To have Vigilo start automatically when you log into your Mac:

  • Open Vigilo's Settings (⚙) → General.
  • Toggle "Launch at Login" on. This uses Apple's native SMAppService framework and requires no administrative privileges.
