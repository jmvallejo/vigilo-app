Privacy Policy

Application: Vigilo: Feed Radar for macOS • Version: 0.1.0+ • Effective: November 6, 2026

Core Promise: Vigilo: Feed Radar is built from the ground up as a private, local-first intelligence radar. We do not collect, store, sell, or track your personal information, feeds, or reading history. There are no analytics servers, no user accounts, and zero tracking SDKs.

1. Overview and Philosophy

Vigilo: Feed Radar ("the Application") is developed by Juan Manuel Vallejo ("we", "us", or "our") as a native desktop utility for macOS. Vigilo: Feed Radar monitors continuous information streams (such as RSS, Atom feeds, Reddit, and X/Twitter) and evaluates them against your custom semantic criteria prompts.

Because we believe information consumption should be private and sovereign, Vigilo operates without a central cloud backend. Your feeds, radar prompts, article archives, vector embeddings, and preferences never pass through our servers because we do not operate any tracking or user-data servers.

3. Stage 1 Filtering: 100% On-Device Embeddings

Vigilo uses an embedded vector embedding engine (fastembed-rs with the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 neural network model) to convert article headlines and your criteria prompts into 384-dimensional mathematical vectors.

This process happens 100% offline directly on your Mac's CPU/GPU via ONNX Runtime. The model weights are downloaded once directly from open-source repositories (Hugging Face) to your local sandboxed cache directory. No text, headlines, or radar queries are ever transmitted across the internet during vector embedding generation.

4. Stage 2 Filtering: AI Briefings & Provider Flexibility

Vigilo allows you to summarize and evaluate matching articles using an AI provider of your choice:

  • Built-In Local AI (Candle / GGUF): Runs entirely offline on your Mac using Apple Metal GPU acceleration. Model weights (such as Gemma 3) are stored locally in your sandbox container. Zero network requests are made.
  • Local Ollama: Connects over local HTTP (http://127.0.0.1:11434) to an Ollama server running on your own machine or local network. Zero data leaves your network.
  • Cloud Providers (Google Gemini & OpenAI — Optional / BYOK): If and only if you explicitly choose a cloud provider and enter your personal API key ("Bring Your Own Key"), candidate article snippets and your radar criteria prompt are sent directly over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections to that provider's official API endpoint (e.g., generativelanguage.googleapis.com or api.openai.com) to generate summaries. Your API keys are encrypted at rest on disk with AES-256-GCM.

5. Ingestion of Third-Party Feeds & Services

To retrieve updates, Vigilo sends direct outbound HTTP requests from your Mac to the public RSS, Atom, and Reddit endpoints you configure. If you provide an optional X (Twitter) Bearer Token, Vigilo contacts the official X API v2 to retrieve timelines and topic searches on your behalf.

These requests are subject to the privacy policies and terms of the respective third-party publishers and platforms. Vigilo does not proxy, intercept, or log these requests.

6. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

All application state is stored locally inside an embedded SQLite database located within your macOS sandboxed container:

~/Library/Containers/com.juanmanuelvallejo.vigilo/Data/Library/Application Support/com.juanmanuelvallejo.vigilo/

  • Automatic Retention: Vigilo includes a user-configurable pruning setting (7, 14, 30, 60, 90 days, or Never) to automatically discard raw, unmatched articles to keep your local database lean. Saved articles and matched briefings are never pruned automatically.
  • User Deletion: You can delete any source, folder, radar, or article at any time directly in the user interface.
  • Total Removal: Dragging Vigilo to the Trash and deleting its Application Support container completely and irreversibly removes all data from your computer.

7. macOS App Sandbox & System Permissions

Vigilo runs under the strict macOS App Sandbox. It only requests the minimal entitlements required to operate:

  • com.apple.security.network.client: Required strictly to poll the feeds you add, download open model weights, and contact your chosen AI APIs.
  • com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write: Used only when you explicitly open a native file picker to export or restore a backup.
  • com.apple.security.cs.allow-jit: Required by Apple Silicon Metal, Candle, and ONNX Runtime for neural network vector calculation.

8. Children's Privacy

Vigilo does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in macOS regulations or new software capabilities. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
