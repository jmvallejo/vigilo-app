The Dual-Stage Intelligence Pipeline

Most readers flood you with hundreds of unread articles every day. Vigilo reverses the paradigm: it operates silently and only alerts you when a piece of information strictly matches your radar.

01

Polymorphic Ingestion

Vigilo polls your configured sources in the background. Supports standard RSS/Atom feeds, Reddit subreddits with automatic rate-limit backoff, and X/Twitter API v2 streams.

RSS 2.0 / Atom Reddit .rss X API v2 SQLite WAL
02

Stage 1: Local ONNX Embeddings

Incoming articles are embedded into 384-dimensional mathematical vectors directly on your Mac using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. High-speed cosine similarity rejects noise in milliseconds with 0 cloud cost.

100% Offline FastEmbed-rs ONNX Runtime Zero Telemetry
03

Stage 2: AI Executive Briefing

Articles exceeding your threshold are dispatched to your chosen AI provider (Built-in Candle Gemma 3, Ollama, Gemini Flash, or OpenAI). Produces a crisp 2-sentence executive summary and urgency rating.

Candle (Metal GPU) Ollama Local Google Gemini OpenAI BYOK
